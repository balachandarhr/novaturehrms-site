Getting started guide

Go live in 8 simple steps – about 30 minutes with your employee list ready in Excel. Templates: download CSV templates.

1. Create your account

Open the app or the web portal and tap New company? Start a free trial. Enter your official email and a strong password, then sign in.

2. Start the free trial

Tap Start Free 7-Day Company Trial and enter your company name. You become the Company Admin and land on this dashboard.

3. Follow the setup checklist

Tap Finish company setup. The steps are numbered and the next one is highlighted. Each step opens the right CSV upload.

4. Upload CSV files in order

Download each sample CSV (01 → 08), replace the sample rows with your data (keep the headings), save as CSV UTF-8 and upload. Mistakes are listed by row number and nothing is saved until the file is clean.

5. Share logins with employees

Employee logins are created automatically from CSV 03 with the temporary password novature@123. Employees must set a new password at first login, then punch in, apply leave and view payslips.

6. Approve requests daily

The Approvals inbox collects leave, attendance corrections, profile changes and expense claims. Tick to approve; employees are notified.

7. Post announcements & holidays

Use Announce and Holidays so everyone sees the notice board and calendar on their home screen.

8. Month end: lock, process, publish

More → Attendance reconciliation & lock → Payroll processing → publish payslips. Export registers to Excel from Reports.
