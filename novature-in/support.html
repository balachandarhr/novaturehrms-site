Support

Last updated: 2 November 2026

We help companies and employees use Novature HRMS.

Employees

For login, attendance, leave or payslip questions, contact your company HR administrator first. Forgot your password? Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen.

Company administrators

Email info@novature.in with your company code, a description and screenshots. See the setup guide.

Response times

We reply within 2 working days (Mon–Sat, 10:00–18:00 IST).

Complaints & grievances

Write to info@novature.in with the subject “Grievance”. We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 15 days.
