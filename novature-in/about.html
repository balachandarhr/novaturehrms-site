About Novature HRMS

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Novature HRMS is a corporate human-resource management system built in India by Novature for small and mid-sized companies.

What it does

  • Attendance with QR gate scan and GPS geofence
  • Leave, comp-off and two-level approvals
  • Payroll with PF/ESI/PT and branded payslips
  • Employee self-service portal and announcements
  • Reports and a guided company setup with CSV checks
  • Multi-company isolation: every company’s data is kept separate

How to start

Companies register on our website for a 7-day free trial and manage their subscription there. Employees then sign in with the Android app or web app.
